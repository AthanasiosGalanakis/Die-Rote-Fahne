# Archived content

Scenes here are not built into the game. `cards/` holds the 1923 cards of the
first versions, taken out in version 0.6 while the cards are rewritten. To bring
one back, move its file into `source/scenes/rote_fahne/cards/` and give it the
tag `rf_party` (the old tags `rf_mass` and `rf_moscow` belonged to decks that
were merged into Party Affairs).
